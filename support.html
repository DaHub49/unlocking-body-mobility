Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase?
Open the app, open any locked exercise or setting to bring up the paywall, and tap Restore Purchases.

Where is my cardio data stored?
All workout logs and assessment records are stored securely on your local device.

Privacy Policy

Unlocking Body Mobility values your privacy:

  • We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information or health metrics.
  • All workout logs, times, and notes are stored strictly on your device using local storage.
  • In-app purchases are securely processed by Apple via StoreKit. We do not have access to your payment card details.
